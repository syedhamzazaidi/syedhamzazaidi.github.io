Export notes
  • Carousel slides export at one identical size, named in swipe order.
  • Stories & Reels use 1080×1920; safe-zone guides are editor-only and never exported.
  • Video uses MP4 when your browser supports it, otherwise WebM or a still poster.
  • Direct posting needs a server, so Swipe Studio hands off to your share sheet or downloads.
